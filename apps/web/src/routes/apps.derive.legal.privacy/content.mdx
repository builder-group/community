# Privacy notice for Dérive

_Effective November 1, 2026_

This notice covers the Dérive iOS app and email support. Visits to builder.group, including the app's landing, help, and legal pages, are covered by the separate [website privacy notice](/legal/privacy/).

## Using the app

Dérive stores photo copies, challenge selections, progress and history, associated timestamps, and onboarding status on your device. Photo processing and grid creation run locally. The app does not change the originals you select from your photo library. CloudKit sync is disabled.

We provide these local functions under your control. The app does not upload your photos, grids, challenge history, or local usage records to us. We do not operate user accounts, advertising, cross-app tracking, or an analytics service for the app. We act as controller for correspondence you send us.

Storage and access strictly necessary for local functions you explicitly request rely on Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG.

## Permissions and choices

You choose existing photos through Apple's photo picker or allow camera access to take new ones. Saving a completed grid to Photos requires permission to add photos. You can manage camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings.

## Retention and deletion

Challenge history and photo copies remain until you delete them. You can delete completed grids and their in-app photo copies from the app. Deleting the app and its data removes its local storage. This does not delete original photos or grids saved to Photos.

We cannot remotely access or delete information stored only on your device. Exports, shared copies, and device backups are separate from the app's local storage.

Selecting photos stored in iCloud may require an Apple-managed download. App data may be included in device backups, depending on your settings. Apple manages these services under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
