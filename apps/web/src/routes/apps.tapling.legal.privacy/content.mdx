# Privacy notice for Tapling

_Effective November 1, 2026_

This notice covers the Tapling iOS app, its keyboard extension, and email support. Visits to builder.group are covered by the separate [website privacy notice](/legal/privacy/).

## Using the app

Tapling stores keyboard preferences, character customizations, collectibles, reward progress, and typing-session records locally. The keyboard and main app share settings and session records through a local app-group store. The stores are configured without CloudKit sync.

Reward session records contain a session identifier, timing information, and a keystroke count rather than the words typed. Autocomplete and autocorrect process text locally using iOS language tools. Suggestions also use an iOS-provided supplementary lexicon, which can include individual first or last names from Contacts, user-defined text shortcuts, and common dictionary words.

We provide these local functions under your control. The app does not upload your typed text, keyboard settings, or local reward records to us. We do not operate user accounts, advertising, cross-app tracking, or an analytics service for the app. We act as controller for correspondence you send us.

Storage and access strictly necessary for local functions you explicitly request rely on Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG.

## Permissions and choices

Full Access enables the keyboard's local sharing of settings and typing-session counts with the main app for rewards. You can continue without Full Access and change it in iOS Settings. You can also disable Earn Keycaps While Typing and change autocomplete and autocorrect settings in the app.

## Retention and deletion

During subsequent session processing, Tapling removes processed session records more than seven days after they were processed. Cumulative counts, reward progress, collectibles, and preferences remain in local storage. Deleting the app and its data removes its local app and keyboard storage.

We cannot remotely access or delete information stored only on your device. Device backups are separate from the app's local storage. Apple manages App Store distribution and any Apple-managed backups under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
