# Privacy notice for SelfieMirror

_Effective November 1, 2026_

This notice covers the SelfieMirror iPhone app and email support. Visits to builder.group, including the app's landing, help, and legal pages, are covered by the separate [website privacy notice](/legal/privacy/).

## Using the app

SelfieMirror stores imported photo copies, project edits, and preferences on your iPhone. Editing and mirror detection run on the device. The app does not change the originals in your photo library.

We provide these local functions under your control. The app does not upload your photos, videos, projects, or local usage records to us. We do not operate user accounts, advertising, cross-app tracking, or an analytics service for the app. We act as controller for correspondence you send us.

Storage and access strictly necessary for local functions you explicitly request rely on Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG.

## Permissions and choices

You choose photos through Apple's photo picker. Saving an exported video or aligned photos to Photos requires your permission, which you can manage in iPhone Settings. Sharing an export sends it to the app or service you choose, which handles its copy under its own privacy arrangements.

The Get Help email draft includes the app version, build, and iOS version. You can review and edit the draft before sending it. The Send Feedback draft does not automatically include those diagnostics.

## Retention and deletion

Projects remain until you delete them. Deleting a project removes its imported copies and edits. Deleting the app and its data removes its local storage. This does not delete exports saved to Photos or copies shared elsewhere.

We cannot remotely access or delete information stored only on your device.

Selecting photos stored in iCloud may require an Apple-managed download. App data may be included in device backups, depending on your settings. Apple manages these services under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
