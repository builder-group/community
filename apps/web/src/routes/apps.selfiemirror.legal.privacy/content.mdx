# Privacy Policy

_Effective October 31, 2026_

SelfieMirror is provided by Benno Kohrs, a sole proprietor in Germany operating as builder.group. This policy covers the iPhone app and its website and support pages. Contact: [support@builder.group](mailto:support@builder.group).

## Using the app

SelfieMirror does not collect or send us your photos, videos, projects or app usage. Editing and mirror detection run on your iPhone. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking or analytics.

When you choose photos, the app stores copies, project edits and preferences on your phone. It does not change the originals in your photo library. Selecting photos stored in iCloud may require a download handled by Apple.

Saving exports to Photos requires your permission. Sharing sends an export only to the app or service you choose. Those services handle the shared copy under their own policies. You can manage Photos permissions in iPhone Settings.

## Storage and deletion

Projects remain on your phone until you delete them. Delete a project from the project library to remove its imported copies and edits. Deleting the app and its data through iPhone Settings removes its local data. This does not delete exports saved to Photos or copies shared elsewhere.

App data may be included in device backups, depending on your settings. Apple manages Photos, iCloud and backups under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you send, including any attachments. The app's help email draft includes the app and iOS versions, which you can review before sending.

We use this information to answer your request and resolve support issues, based on our legitimate interest in helping users. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose or required by law. You can request deletion by email. We do not sell your information or use support messages for advertising.

## Visiting the website

These pages use Vercel hosting, Vercel Web Analytics and Google Fonts. Delivering pages and fonts involves technical request data such as your IP address and browser information. Web Analytics provides aggregate information about visits, such as pages viewed, browser type and approximate location. This website processing is separate from the iPhone app.

See [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice), [Web Analytics privacy information](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details, including processing locations and retention. The website also remembers your display theme locally in your browser.

## Your choices and rights

Contact [support@builder.group](mailto:support@builder.group) to ask about personal information you have sent us, or to request access, correction or deletion. Where applicable, you can also request a copy, restrict processing or object to it, and complain to a data protection authority. We cannot access projects stored only on your phone.

We will publish updates to this policy here with a new effective date.
