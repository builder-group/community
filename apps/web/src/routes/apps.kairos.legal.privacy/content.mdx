# Privacy notice for Kairos

_Effective November 1, 2026_

This notice covers the Kairos iOS app and email support. Visits to builder.group, including the app's landing, help, and legal pages, are covered by the separate [website privacy notice](/legal/privacy/).

## Using the app

Kairos stores timer settings, labels, recent timer configurations, current timer state, and appearance preferences on your device. Alarms and sounds are handled locally.

We provide these local functions under your control. The app does not upload your timer settings, labels, or local usage records to us. We do not operate user accounts, advertising, cross-app tracking, or an analytics service for the app. We act as controller for correspondence you send us.

Storage and access strictly necessary for local functions you explicitly request rely on Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG.

## Permissions and choices

Kairos requests notification permission to alert you when a timer finishes in the background. These are local notifications scheduled on your device. You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

## Retention and deletion

You can remove individual recent timers or clear the recent list. Reset App restores timer settings and preferences and clears recents. Deleting the app and its data removes its local storage.

We cannot remotely access or delete information stored only on your device. Device backups are separate from the app's local storage. Apple manages App Store distribution and any Apple-managed backups under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
