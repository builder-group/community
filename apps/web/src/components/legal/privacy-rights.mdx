## Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you can request access to, correction or erasure of your personal data, restriction of processing, and data portability, subject to the conditions for those rights.

### Right to object

Where processing is based on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

### Withdrawal of consent

Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

### Requests and complaints

Contact [support@builder.group](mailto:support@builder.group) to exercise your rights. We process these requests to fulfil our GDPR obligations under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

You can complain to a supervisory authority, particularly in the EU member state of your habitual residence, workplace, or the alleged infringement. Our competent authority is the [Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA)](https://www.lda.bayern.de/).
