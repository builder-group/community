## Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments. Providing this information is optional, but some details may be needed to answer your request.

We use correspondence to answer enquiries and provide support. Our legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and our legitimate interest in responding to these messages. Where processing is necessary to perform a contract with you or take pre-contractual steps you request, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies.

We use Google Workspace (Gmail) to receive and store email. Google processes message data on our behalf and may process it outside the EEA, including in the USA. Google's [transfer terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms/alternative-transfer-solution) provide for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered US transfers and contractual safeguards under its [Cloud Data Processing Addendum](https://cloud.google.com/terms/data-processing-addendum/).

We archive email correspondence indefinitely, without an automatic deletion schedule.
